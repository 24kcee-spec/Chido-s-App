# CAC 4203: Advanced Auditing
**Level:** Part IV (Final Year - Second Semester)  
**Department:** Accounting  
**Prerequisite:** CAC 4103 Audit Skills, Theory and Practice  

---

## 📌 Course Description
Builds upon foundational auditing principles to tackle specialized assurance engagements, forensic accounting, environmental auditing, public sector audit frameworks, and complex reporting under International Standards on Auditing (ISA).

---

## 📚 Core Modules & Syllabus Breakdown

### Unit 1: Specialized Assurance Engagements
- **ISA 800 (Revised):** Audits of financial statements prepared in accordance with special purpose frameworks.
- **ISA 805 (Revised):** Audits of single financial statements and specific elements, accounts, or items.
- **ISA 810 (Revised):** Engagements to report on summary financial statements.
- **ISRE 2400 / 2410:** Review of interim financial information performed by the independent auditor.
- **ISAE 3402:** Assurance reports on controls at a service organization (SOC 1 / SOC 2).

### Unit 2: Forensic Auditing & Fraud Investigations
- The Fraud Triangle: Incentive/Pressure, Opportunity, and Rationalization.
- Investigating asset misappropriation, fraudulent financial reporting, and bribery/corruption.
- Digital forensics: Preserving chain of custody, data extraction, Benford's Law analysis.
- Serving as an expert witness in civil and criminal commercial litigation.

### Unit 3: Public Sector & Value for Money (VFM) Auditing
- INTOSAI Auditing Standards and Public Finance Management Act (PFMA).
- The 3Es of VFM Audit: Economy, Efficiency, and Effectiveness.
- Auditor General of Zimbabwe statutory mandate and audit of state-owned enterprises (parastatals).

### Unit 4: ESG & Sustainability Assurance
- ISSA 5000 (General Requirements for Sustainability Assurance Engagements).
- Auditing greenhouse gas emissions, carbon credits, and corporate social responsibility reports.

### Unit 5: Corporate Governance & Legal Liability
- Corporate Governance Codes (National Code on Corporate Governance Zimbabwe - ZIMCODE).
- Audit Committee responsibilities: Overseeing financial reporting, internal controls, and auditor independence.
- Auditor common law liability: Duty of care to clients vs third parties (*Hedley Byrne*, *Caparo Industries v Dickman*).
