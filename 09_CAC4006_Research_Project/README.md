# CAC 4006: Research Project (Dissertation)
**Level:** Part IV (Final Year - Across Semesters 1 & 2)  
**Department:** Accounting  
**Assessment:** Final Dissertation & Oral Defense  

---

## 📌 Course Overview
The Final Year Research Project (CAC 4006) is a capstone independent academic dissertation requiring students to identify an empirical or theoretical research problem in Accounting, Auditing, Taxation, Corporate Governance, or Financial Management.

---

## 📑 Typical Accounting Research Topics
1. **Adoption & Compliance with IFRS:** Impact of IFRS 16 on gearing and financial performance of Zimbabwe Stock Exchange (ZSE) listed firms.
2. **Tax Compliance & Digital Economy:** Challenges of presumptive tax collection and IMTT on informal and SME business operations in Zimbabwe.
3. **Audit Quality & Corporate Governance:** The effect of board independence and audit committee expertise on corporate reporting credibility.
4. **Accounting Information Systems & Cyber Risk:** Evaluating internal controls in mobile money and electronic banking transaction processing.
5. **Sustainability & ESG Reporting:** Extent of climate-related financial disclosures among mining and agricultural enterprises.

---

## 🏗️ Dissertation Structure (Standard 5 Chapters)

### Chapter 1: Introduction
- Background to the study
- Problem statement
- Research objectives & corresponding research questions
- Significance/Justification of the study
- Scope and limitations
- Operational definition of terms

### Chapter 2: Literature Review
- Theoretical framework (e.g., Agency Theory, Stakeholder Theory, Legitimacy Theory, Fraud Triangle)
- Conceptual review & empirical literature review
- Gaps in existing local and regional research

### Chapter 3: Research Methodology
- Research design (Descriptive, Correlational, Explanatory)
- Population, target sample, and sampling techniques (Purposive vs Stratified)
- Data sources: Primary data (questionnaires, interviews) & Secondary data (published annual reports)
- Reliability & validity tests (Cronbach's alpha)
- Data analysis tools (SPSS, Stata, Excel regression models)
- Ethical considerations

### Chapter 4: Data Presentation, Analysis & Discussion
- Demographic and sample response rates
- Descriptive statistics (Mean, Standard Deviation, Skewness)
- Inferential statistics (Regression analysis, Correlation matrix, ANOVA)
- Hypotheses testing and discussion of findings against literature

### Chapter 5: Summary, Conclusions & Recommendations
- Summary of main empirical findings
- Academic and practical policy conclusions
- Recommendations for ZIMRA, PAAB, ICAZ, corporate boards, or policymakers
- Suggestions for further research
