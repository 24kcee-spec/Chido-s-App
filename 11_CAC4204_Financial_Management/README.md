# CAC 4204: Financial Management
**Level:** Part IV (Final Year - Second Semester)  
**Department:** Accounting  
**Focus:** Corporate Finance & Strategic Financial Decisions  

---

## 📌 Course Description
Advanced financial management principles covering strategic investment appraisal, cost of capital, capital structure theories, corporate valuation for mergers and acquisitions, dividend policy, and international treasury risk management.

---

## 📚 Core Modules & Syllabus Breakdown

### Unit 1: Strategic Investment Appraisal
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF): Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), and Modified IRR (MIRR).
- Inflation and taxation adjustments: Capital allowances tax shield and nominal vs real discount rates.
- Project risk and uncertainty: Sensitivity analysis, Scenario analysis, and Simulation.
- Capital rationing: Single-period (Profitability Index) vs Multi-period (Linear programming).
- Asset replacement decisions and Equivalent Annual Cost (EAC).

### Unit 2: Cost of Capital & Capital Structure
- **Cost of Equity (Ke):** Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) and Dividend Valuation Model (Gordon Growth).
- **Cost of Debt (Kd):** Redeemable vs irredeemable debt, convertible debentures.
- **Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC):** Formula, weights (market value vs book value), and assumptions.
- **Capital Structure Theories:**
  - Traditional view (optimal gearing exists).
  - Modigliani & Miller (1958) without tax: Gearing is irrelevant.
  - Modigliani & Miller (1963) with tax: Debt tax shield favors maximum gearing.
  - Trade-Off Theory (tax shield vs financial distress costs).
  - Pecking Order Theory (Retained earnings → Debt → Equity).

### Unit 3: Business Valuation, Mergers & Acquisitions
- Asset-based valuation (Net asset value, replacement cost, realizable value).
- Income/Earnings valuation: Price-Earnings (P/E) ratio, EV/EBITDA multiples, and Dividend Yield.
- Discounted Cash Flow (Free Cash Flow to Firm / FCFF & Free Cash Flow to Equity / FCFE).
- Financing acquisitions: Cash vs Share-for-share exchange, earn-outs.
- Defense tactics against hostile takeovers (Poison pills, White knights, Staggered boards).

### Unit 4: Foreign Exchange Risk Management
- Currency risks: Transaction risk, Translation risk, and Economic risk.
- Internal hedging techniques: Invoicing in home currency, leading and lagging, multilateral netting.
- External hedging instruments: Forward exchange contracts, Money market hedges, Currency futures, and Currency options.
- Interest rate parity (IRP) and Purchasing power parity (PPP).
