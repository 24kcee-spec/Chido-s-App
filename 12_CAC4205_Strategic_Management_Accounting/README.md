# CAC 4205: Strategic Management Accounting
**Level:** Part IV (Final Year - Second Semester)  
**Department:** Accounting  
**Prerequisite:** CAC 4105 Management and Cost Accounting II  

---

## 📌 Course Description
Integrates management accounting techniques with strategic positioning, competitive advantage, and performance evaluation in contemporary operating environments.

---

## 📚 Core Modules & Syllabus Breakdown

### Unit 1: Strategic Cost Management Techniques
- **Target Costing & Value Engineering:** Setting selling price from market demand, deducting desired margin, deriving target cost, and closing cost gaps through product design.
- **Life-Cycle Costing:** Accounting for research, design, production, marketing, and decommissioning costs over entire product lifecycle.
- **Kaizen Costing:** Continuous incremental improvements during manufacturing phase.
- **Environmental Management Accounting (EMA):** Identifying, allocating, and controlling environmental and material waste costs.

### Unit 2: Operations & Capacity Accounting
- **Just-In-Time (JIT) & Backflush Accounting:** Eliminating buffer inventory, kanban signals, and simplified accounting entries.
- **Theory of Constraints & Throughput Accounting (TA):** Identifying bottlenecks, calculating Throughput Accounting Ratio (TPAR = Return per factory hour / Cost per factory hour).
- **Total Quality Management (TQM):** Cost of Quality categories (Prevention, Appraisal, Internal Failure, External Failure).

### Unit 3: Activity-Based Cost Management (ABM)
- Customer Profitability Analysis (CPA) and customer lifetime value.
- Direct Product Profitability (DPP) in retail and wholesale distribution.
- Activity-Based Budgeting (ABB) vs Traditional Incremental Budgeting.

### Unit 4: Strategic Performance Measurement
- **The Balanced Scorecard (Kaplan & Norton):** Financial, Customer, Internal Business Processes, and Learning & Growth perspectives.
- **Building Block Model (Fitzgerald & Moon):** Dimensions, Standards, and Rewards for service businesses.
- **Beyond Budgeting:** Decentralization, rolling forecasts, relative targets, and dynamic resource allocation.
